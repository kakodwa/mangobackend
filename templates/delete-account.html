MalaTrade – Account Deletion Request

MalaTrade respects your privacy and gives users the right to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your MalaTrade account, please send an email to:

Email: support@malatrade.com

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request

Required Verification: Include the email address or phone number associated with your MalaTrade account so we can verify your ownership and process the request safely.

Compose Deletion Request Email

Information That Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified and processed, we will permanently delete or anonymize eligible personal information, including:

  • Account profile information.
  • Personal contact details.
  • Uploaded profile images.
  • Personal preferences and associated account data.

Information That May Be Retained

Some transaction, payment, order, booking, or financial records may be retained where required by applicable laws, regulatory requirements, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

Deletion Processing Period

Verified deletion requests will normally be processed within 30 days, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
