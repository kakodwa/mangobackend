Order Processing & Escrow Release Flow:

  1. Wait for Payment Confirmation: Prepare the items now, but only dispatch the order after payment is marked completed on your dashboard.
  2. Generating Customer Delivery Code: Go to your seller dashboard under Shop Management → Deliveries & Orders. Assign your courier/rider and update the order status to "In Transit". This automatically sends the customer their unique Delivery Code.
  3. Share Your Seller Code with the Courier/Rider: Share your unique Seller Code with your dispatch courier or rider.
  4. Releasing Funds from Escrow: Upon delivering the package, your courier/rider enters your Seller Code in the app (located on the Top Nav Bar on Desktop or inside the Side Drawer Menu on Mobile). This opens the verification screen where they enter the Customer's Delivery Code to instantly release payment from escrow into your wallet!

Log in to your seller dashboard to track payment status, assign delivery riders, and view active orders.
