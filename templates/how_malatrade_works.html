How MalaTrade Works

MalaTrade makes it easy to shop, sell, and connect in Malawi.

For Buyers

Create Account → Browse Products → Add to Cart → Checkout → Pay → Receive Your Order → Confirm Delivery

For Sellers

Create Account → Create Shop → Get Verified → Add Products → Receive Orders → Deliver Products → Get Paid → Withdraw Earnings

1 Create Your MalaTrade Account

Creating a MalaTrade account is completely free. Get started in four simple steps:

  1. Click Login
  2. Click Register
  3. Fill in the registration form
  4. Click Submit
Important: You do not need to create a shop to buy products. A regular MalaTrade account is enough to start shopping immediately.
[IMAGE: Registration & Login Screen]

2 Create Your Shop & Add Products

If you want to sell, open your store in two easy ways:

Option 1: From Top Navigation

Click the Create Shop button on the header after logging in.

Option 2: From Profile

Go to Profile → Shop Management → Create Shop.

Once verified by MalaTrade, navigate to Add Products to set up item descriptions, prices, images, and variations (e.g., shoe sizes).

[IMAGE: Seller Dashboard & Add Product Interface]

3 Browse, Select & Checkout

Buying requires no shop account. Browse items via categories, choose desired variations (e.g., Size 7–10), and add them to your cart.

Entering Delivery Details: At checkout, input your name, phone number, physical address, and GPS Location to pinpoint your exact location for courier/rider delivery.

[IMAGE: Cart & GPS Delivery Address Screen]

4 Secure Payment

All transactions are powered securely by PayChangu.

Supported Payment Options:

Airtel Money TNM Mpamba Visa / Mastercard

For mobile money, enter your details and confirm the PIN prompt on your phone. Card payments securely redirect to the PayChangu payment gateway.

[IMAGE: PayChangu Checkout Portal Interface]

5 Order Tracking & Escrow Delivery

Track status under Profile → My Activity → Orders (Statuses: Order Placed, Processing, Ready, In Transit, Delivered).

How Escrow Protects You:

When you pay, MalaTrade holds funds safely. Payments are only released to the seller after you provide your unique **Delivery Confirmation Code** to the delivery agent upon receiving your items.

Note: If items are received without manually providing the code, funds release automatically after 2 days per protocol.

[IMAGE: Delivery Confirmation Code Verification Screen]

6 Seller Fulfillments & Withdrawals

Sellers handle orders from Profile → Shop Management → Orders & Deliveries. Using their seller delivery code, customer delivery details and navigation routes become accessible to complete delivery.

Once confirmed, earnings are deposited to your wallet. Request payouts anytime under Profile → Payments & Wallet → Withdraw Money.

[IMAGE: Seller Wallet & Withdrawal Screen]
