2. Delivery & Escrow Protection Policy

🔒 Safe Escrow & Delivery Confirmation Mechanism

To protect both buyers and sellers across MalaTrade, all product purchases follow a strict escrow protection protocol:

  • Funds Protection: When a customer buys an item on MalaTrade, the payment is safely held by MalaTrade and not instantly transferred to the seller.
  • Delivery Handover: The seller or courier/rider is responsible for delivering the physical item to the customer.
  • Delivery Confirmation Code: Upon receiving the item, the customer is required to provide their unique Delivery Code to the seller, courier, or rider. Alternatively, the seller requests this code from the customer at the point of delivery.
  • Funds Release upon Confirmation: Once the seller enters and confirms the Delivery Code in the MalaTrade app/platform, the payment is immediately unlocked and credited to the seller's wallet.
  • Automatic 2-Day Release Clause: If the physical delivery has taken place but the customer fails or forgets to provide/confirm the Delivery Code within 48 hours (2 days), MalaTrade will automatically confirm the delivery and release the funds to the seller.
