Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

At MalaTrade, available via malatrade.com and our mobile applications, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, process, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & WhatsApp Data: Name, email address, profile photo, and WhatsApp phone number formatted with an international country code (e.g., +265, +260).
  • Delivery & Order Verification Data: Delivery addresses, rider tracking statuses, and secret Delivery Confirmation Codes used to manage secure order releases.
  • Geolocation Information: Precise GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) used to pin shop locations and enable geographical searching.
  • Financial & Escrow Logs: Wallet balances, cashout requests, transaction histories, and ticket/lodge payment checkout logs.
  • Hardware Access: Camera access granted explicitly for QR code verification (tickets/bookings check-in) and cropping photo uploads.

2. How We Process Delivery & Escrow Information

Order Tracking & Escrow Logs: MalaTrade processes order and delivery code verification details solely to protect transaction funds. Payments are securely held in escrow until a delivery code is verified or automatically triggered after the standard 2-day delivery confirmation window.

3. Use of Personal Data

Your information is utilized strictly to:

  • Facilitate buyer-seller trade communication through direct calls or WhatsApp links.
  • Authorize payouts, wallet withdrawals, and order fulfillment verification.
  • Enable interactive map navigation for store locations.
  • Prevent fraud, manage system performance, and deliver support services.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used strictly for scanning event/lodge QR codes and capturing shop/product media.
  • Location: Used for store mapping and proximity-based listing discoveries.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ encrypted server connections, secure payment gateways, and protected database architectures to keep your account details and escrow balance logs safe.

6. Contact Us

For privacy queries, account deletion requests, or data updates, please contact us at privacy@malatrade.com or through the Help & Support module in the app.
